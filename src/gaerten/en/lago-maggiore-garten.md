    Modern Mediterranean Garden at Lago Maggiore | Ticino Garden Design for a Hillside Property

    This residential garden on Lago Maggiore was designed to connect contemporary architecture with the steep, sun-exposed landscape of Ticino. Through terracing, natural stone retaining walls, climate-resilient planting and detailed site supervision, the project transformed a challenging hillside plot into a series of outdoor living spaces with uninterrupted lake views.

    Garden Design for a Steep Hillside Site

    The property is shaped by a steep slope, intense sun exposure, and the specific climatic conditions of the Lago Maggiore region. Our landscape architecture approach focused on turning these challenges into defining qualities of the design.

    Dry-stone retaining walls made from local natural stone structure the terrain and stabilise the hillside. Carefully positioned terraces create distinct outdoor spaces for dining, lounging, and circulation while maintaining open views of the lake and surrounding mountains. Integrated drainage solutions support long-term stability and manage heavy seasonal rainfall typical of the Prealpine landscape.

    Planting Design for the Ticino Microclimate

    The planting concept follows a naturalistic design approach inspired by the New Perennial movement and adapted to the mild Mediterranean microclimate of Ascona and Locarno.

    Evergreen planting provides year-round structure, privacy, and architectural clarity. Perennials and ornamental grasses add seasonal change, movement, and texture throughout the year. Drought-tolerant species were selected to reduce irrigation needs, strengthen climate resilience, and ensure the garden remains robust and attractive over time.

    Landscape Architecture and Project Execution

    Atelier Wehrle provided full design coordination and site supervision for the project. This included the collaboration with specialist stone masons, irrigation experts, and nurseries to ensure that every element of the garden was executed with precision and in line with the overall design concept.

    The result is a contemporary garden on Lago Maggiore that brings together architecture, topography, planting, and craftsmanship in one coherent outdoor space.
