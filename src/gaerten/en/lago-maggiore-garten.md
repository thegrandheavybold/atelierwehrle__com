    A terraced villa garden above Lake Maggiore, shaped by dramatic topography and long views. On a site only 16 meters deep, a height difference of around ten meters was transformed into a dynamic sequence of outdoor rooms with views across the lake and the Swiss Alps.
    The entrance zone feels calm and elegant, with semi-shade planting including peonies, Tetrapanax, Clematis armandii, Osmanthus, ferns, camellias and Acanthus mollis. A mature magnolia anchors the space and creates a sense of long-established character.
    Around the pool terrace, a Mediterranean, climate-resilient planting concept connects architecture and landscape. Cork oak, Tulbaghia violacea, Gaura lindheimeri, Trachelospermum jasminoides and Romneya coulteri add lightness and summer expression.
    The main terrace is shaded by a wisteria pergola, a classic Ticino motif, with roses, lavender, hardy lemon trees and Magnolia grandiflora. The upper woodland-inspired zone with palms, pomegranate, fig and pine completes the project as a natural, slightly wild finale.
