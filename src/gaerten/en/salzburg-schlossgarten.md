  copy: |-
    The parterre at Leopoldskron Palace in Salzburg, the former home of Max Reinhardt, co-founder of the Salzburg Festival, combines historical grandeur with timeless elegance. The palace, which also served as the setting for a Chanel fashion show under Karl Lagerfeld, inspired me to redesign the garden.

          The design incorporates the Baroque architecture of the palace, reinterpreted in a contemporary way. Instead of the monotonous gravel parterre, roses and perennials now bloom in vibrant colors. The diseased boxwood was replaced by a special yew tree, giving the garden a fresh structure. Fountains and benches, designed by Manuel Wehrle, harmoniously blend into the overall concept.

          This palace garden demonstrates how tradition and modernity can be combined in a stylish way. Let this design inspire you and discover how a custom-designed garden can enrich your surroundings.
