  copy: |-
    High above Lake Maggiore, this villa garden was designed to frame the view and cool the house in summer. An infinity pool acts like a quiet mirror to the sky, while natural-stone walls and “floating” limestone steps guide you through the site. Olive trees, palms and a mix of blue-flowering perennials bring a soft Mediterranean feel. The planting is drought-tolerant and pollinator-friendly, so it needs little irrigation once established. Gravel joints and permeable paths let rainwater soak into the ground; lighting is subtle to protect night life.

    The architecture stays crisp, the garden stays relaxed. You move from shade to sun, from house to water, with long views over the lake at every turn. It’s a calm, timeless place for everyday living - simple, resilient, and made to last.
