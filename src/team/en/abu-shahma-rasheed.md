Abu comes from northern India. His career in design began with a degree in architecture and the conviction that the real magic happens in the spaces between buildings. After two years working as an architect in India, he moved to Germany to complete his master's degree at Anhalt University of Applied Sciences.
Today, Abu lives in Potsdam and works on drawings that translate design ideas into concrete plans. He is convinced that a public space is successful when it solves a social problem and is visually appealing at the same time.
In his free time, Abu watches cricket, goes to the gym and enjoys travelling with friends. When exploring new cities, he observes closely how people use public spaces – always on the lookout for ideas that make places lively and inviting.
