Barbara's love of gardens was awakened in 1995 at Hidcote Manor in England. Since then, she has been fascinated by landscaped garden spaces and plant compositions. After studying and working in the design of private gardens and as a plant planner in a landscape architecture firm, she has been working at Atelier Wehrle since January 2026. Discovering the special features of gardens and bringing their beauty to life through plants is her passion, which she puts into practice at the studio in plant planning with the signature style of Manuel Wehrle. She also supervises projects in construction management.
In her free time, she indulges her urge to explore plants while hiking and cycling – preferably with friends or like-minded people. Having left the Bergisches Land region, she is looking forward to rediscovering the landscape in the far north. Besides her family, yoga is an important part of her life.
