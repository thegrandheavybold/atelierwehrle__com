Luca grew up close to nature in a small village in North Rhine-Westphalia. He completed his Abitur in Berlin, where he also gained practical experience as an assistant in garden and landscape construction. He later completed a bachelor’s degree in landscape architecture.

His interests include technology and history. In his free time, he skates, plays video games, and engages with art as well as technical subjects. Professionally, he is motivated by shaping nature from an aesthetic perspective.
