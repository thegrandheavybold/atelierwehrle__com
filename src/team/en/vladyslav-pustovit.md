Vladyslav is a filmmaker and cameraman from Ukraine – and a big fan of Excel spreadsheets. He has been fascinated by nature and plants since childhood. Through his collaboration with Mr. Wehrle, he also discovered his passion for garden photography and the special connection between design, atmosphere and landscape. As studio manager, he is responsible not only for organisational and administrative processes, but also for the studio's visual communication. With his eye for visual language, structure and detail, he operates at the interface between organisation and design.
